HydroPet — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

HydroPet — Water Reminder & Pet Buddy is a device-first water-tracking app. It does not require an account, operate its own backend, or upload your water entries to a HydroPet server.

Information you enter

Your water amounts, daily goals, preferred units, glass sizes, reminder times, streak information, and pet seed are stored on your device in Android private SharedPreferences. These entries are used to show your progress, history, pet, and notification schedule. The app does not transmit your water diary to its developer. App data is not backed up by Android cloud backup in this release.

Advertising and third parties

The app includes Google's AdMob advertising SDK and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent SDK. If ads are allowed, these Google services may collect or process information such as device identifiers, approximate location, advertising interactions, diagnostics, and IP address according to Google's policies and applicable consent choices. Ad requests require internet access. HydroPet does not include its diary data, drinking amounts, pet seed, or reminder times in ad requests.

Google privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google advertising information: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

Ads appear only on History and Settings (banner), and potentially when you explicitly continue after unlocking a new growth stage (interstitial, at most once every four hours). There are no rewarded ads, no ads on the water logging screen, and no full-screen ads when launching HydroPet.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Android WorkManager schedules friendly local notifications at your selected times. Notification permission can be denied or revoked in device settings. WorkManager delivery can be delayed due to battery optimizations. No remote push service or notification server is used. The reminder text is generated locally.

Retention and deletion

Your water entries and settings remain on the device unless you delete an entry or remove the app's data in Android Settings / uninstall HydroPet. Removing the app may not remove data already processed by Google's advertising services; consult Google's privacy controls for those categories. You can change reminders and the privacy choices (when required in your region) in the app.

Children and health

This app is for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. HydroPet offers habit tracking only; it is not medical advice and does not determine a medically appropriate amount of water for you. Your needs vary with health, activity, climate, and medical guidance.

Security

The app stores personal progress in app-private storage without any custom backend. No remote account or authentication is provided. Users should secure their devices with a lock screen.
