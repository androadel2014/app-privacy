Snake Worlds — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Snake Worlds is an offline arcade game. No account or server is required, and the app itself does not collect personal data.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (stars, unlocked levels and skins), high scores, Daily Run results and settings are stored only on your device using Android SharedPreferences. We do not operate a gameplay analytics service and do not upload your progress, scores or settings anywhere.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google's Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK. A banner may appear on the menu, world map and results screens only — never during gameplay. A full-screen ad may appear between levels, and an optional rewarded ad may be offered for a single continue. The SDK may process advertising identifiers (where available), IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device information, ad interactions, diagnostics and other standard ad-serving data in accordance with Google's policies. Google and its partners may use this information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and compliance. Consent choices, where required, are requested using Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is loaded.

We do not sell player progress, scores or settings.

In-app purchases

The app offers one optional one-time purchase ("Remove Ads") processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. Purchase records are held by Google Play; a purchase can be restored from inside the app.

Data retention

The game data remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall it. Data held by Google as an advertising and billing provider follows Google's own retention rules.

Children

This app is intended for a general audience; it is not directed at children.

Your choices and contact

You may decline optional advertising consent where offered, purchase Remove Ads to stop ads in the app, or uninstall the app to remove local records. Android device settings may offer advertising privacy controls. If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can contact us at androadel2014@gmail.com.
