Checkout IQ — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Checkout IQ — Smart Shopping & Daily Deals ("the App") is an Android application that helps people compare unit prices, play a daily shopping challenge, and practice price comparisons. The App is designed to function without creating an account or connecting to a Checkout IQ server.

Information stored on your device

The App stores your daily challenge progress, daily result history, streak, practice score, optional bonus unlock state, appearance preference, and the time of the last interstitial ad in Android SharedPreferences on your device. The App does not upload these gameplay records to a developer-operated service. Your price comparison entries are processed on your device; they are not sent to a developer-operated service. Uninstalling or clearing application storage ordinarily removes this information. Android backups are disabled for the App's SharedPreferences.

Advertising, third-party services, and personal data

The App integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display banners on results and history pages, limited interstitial ads at round-set boundaries, and optional rewarded ads. Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information such as IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, app/device identifiers (including the advertising ID where available), device and app interaction data, diagnostics, and information about ad views or clicks to deliver, measure, secure and personalize or otherwise select ads, depending on your settings and applicable law. These third-party services communicate over the internet. We do not control data collected directly by Google through its SDK.

Please review Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

Consent and privacy controls

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent messages when required. Consent status is consulted before requesting ads. Where an ad privacy-options form is required, you may open it from About → Manage ad privacy choices. Privacy and advertising options also depend on your Android and Google device settings.

Sharing

When you choose Share text or Share image, Android displays a share sheet so you can choose a receiving app. The App creates the image locally and shares it only after you initiate that action. Information you share is subject to the privacy practices of the app you select.

No developer accounts or app analytics

The App does not require an account, does not include a developer-operated backend, and does not integrate a separate analytics service. Google advertising services may perform their own measurement and fraud prevention as described above.

Children

The App is intended for a general audience and is not designed specifically for children under 13. Before distribution, the publisher must complete Google Play's audience and content declarations accurately.

Changes

We may update this policy if features or advertising technology change. An updated version should be made available in the app and on the public privacy-policy URL used for the Google Play listing.
