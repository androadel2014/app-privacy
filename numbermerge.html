Number Merge — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Number Merge does not require an account or a server. The saved board, score, best score, undo count, and preferences remain on your device using Android local storage. The game developer does not receive those values. Local game data is removed if you clear app data or uninstall the game. No first-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK is included.

Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, and the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable advertising consent. The current release (version 1.0.0) displays Google test banner ads on the menu screen only. Google's ad technology may collect or process advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, interactions with advertisements, and other standard ad-serving data, subject to consent and applicable law. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized as applicable. Consult Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and Google Ads information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. You may access advertising privacy choices from the About screen when required.

Permissions and connectivity

The game requests no optional device permissions. Internet connectivity may be used by Google's advertising SDK.
