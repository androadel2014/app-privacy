Private Scan — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Your scans and page images are stored in this app's private storage on your device. Scans use the Google Play services ML Kit Document Scanner; text recognition uses the on-device ML Kit Latin text recognition model. Private Scan does not upload your document contents to its own servers. There is no account, cloud sync, subscription, analytics SDK, or watermark.

Sharing, import, and deletion

Your saved document files leave the device only when you intentionally share them using Android's share sheet. Imported photos are copied into private storage. Deleting a document removes the stored PDF and page images from the app's library. Android backups of app files are disabled.

You can remove your saved documents with Delete. Uninstalling the app removes app-private files.

Advertising

Private Scan uses Google AdMob. The current release (version 1.0.0) displays a Google test banner ad on the library screen only. No ads appear during scanning, document viewing, OCR, or sharing. Google may process the advertising ID, device information, IP address, consent choices, and ad interaction/diagnostic data for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention, according to Google's privacy policies. UMP is used to manage applicable consent choices.

Google Play services

Google Play services provides the scanner interface and may process service diagnostics under Google's own policies. Document content is processed locally through the scanning and OCR tools; the app does not send scanned pages to an application backend. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
