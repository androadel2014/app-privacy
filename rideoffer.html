RideOffer Copilot — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

RideOffer Copilot is a private, device-first analyzer for rideshare offer screenshots. It does not require an account, and it does not send your screenshots or your trip records to the developer. RideOffer Copilot is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Uber or Lyft.

On-device screenshot analysis

When you import or share an offer screenshot, the image is read on your phone using Google's ML Kit text recognition, which runs locally on the device. The screenshot image itself is not uploaded anywhere and is not saved by the app. Only the numbers and details extracted from it (such as fare, trip miles and minutes, and pickup and drop-off text) are used to calculate the verdict shown to you.

Text recognition itself runs on your device. The ML Kit software library is provided by Google and may, as part of its normal operation (for example, downloading or updating its on-device recognition model), communicate with Google servers; any such communication does not include your screenshots or trip records from the app. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Information stored on your device

The offer records you create (fare, miles, minutes, the calculated net-per-mile and net-per-hour figures, verdict, your accept/decline outcome, timestamps, and a privacy-sanitized copy of the recognized text), your wait records, and your settings (verdict thresholds, operating cost, driver ID, and preferences) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

Rider names are never stored: the app is designed to drop names from recognized text before anything is saved, and the stored text is sanitized again before it is kept or synced. You can review everything the app has stored in the History screen, export it to a file you choose, or delete all of it from within the app.

Optional sync — off by default

The app includes an optional sync feature that is turned off by default and stays off unless you enable it yourself in Settings. If you enable it, the app sends your stored offer records, addresses, and wait records over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to the server address you enter — your own server — together with the driver key you provide. This is the only circumstance in which record data leaves your phone, and it goes only to the server you configured. Your driver key is stored on the device encrypted using the Android Keystore. You can turn sync off, clear your stored driver key, or delete your history at any time in the app.

Advertising, analytics, and third parties

RideOffer Copilot contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no third-party data collection. Your data is not sold or shared with third parties.

Retention and deletion

Your records and settings remain on your device until you delete them in the app (History → "Delete all history"), clear the app's data in Android Settings, or uninstall the app. If you enabled sync, copies you sent to your own server are under your control on that server.

Children

This app is a work tool for rideshare drivers and is not directed to children under 13.

Security

Records are kept in app-private storage and the app does not provide remote accounts or authentication. You should secure your device with a lock screen.
