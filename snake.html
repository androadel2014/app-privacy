Snake Classic — Pixel LCD — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Snake Classic is an offline arcade game. No account or server is required.

Information stored on your device

Your high scores (including date, selected speed and mode) and settings are stored only on your device using Android SharedPreferences. We do not operate a gameplay analytics service and do not upload scores or settings.

Advertising

The app uses Google's Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK. The current release (version 1.0.0) displays Google test ads on the main menu and game-over screens, plus an optional rewarded revive. No banner or full-screen ad is presented during live gameplay, and a rewarded ad is always optional. The SDK may process advertising identifiers (where available), IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device information, ad interactions, diagnostics and other standard ad-serving data in accordance with Google's policies. Google and its partners may use this information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and compliance. Consent choices, where required, are requested using Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is loaded.

We do not sell player scores or settings.

Data retention

The game data remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall it. Data held by Google as an advertising provider follows Google's own retention rules.

Children

This app is intended for a general audience; it is not directed at children.

Your choices and contact

You may decline optional advertising consent where offered, or uninstall the app to remove local records. Android device settings may offer advertising privacy controls. If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can contact us at androadel2014@gmail.com.
