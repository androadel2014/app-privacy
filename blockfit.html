BlockFit Daily — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

BlockFit Daily is an offline block puzzle game with a daily board. No account or server is required, and the game works fully without a connection.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, scores, daily streaks and settings are stored only on your device. We do not operate a gameplay analytics service and do not upload your scores, streaks or settings anywhere.

Advertising

The app uses Google's Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK. Ads appear only on the Home and Results screens — never on the game screen — and full-screen ads are capped and never shown during play, exactly as promised in the app's published ad contract. The SDK may process advertising identifiers (where available), IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device information, ad interactions, diagnostics and other standard ad-serving data in accordance with Google's policies. Google and its partners may use this information for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and compliance. Consent choices, where required, are requested using Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is loaded.

Purchases

The app offers one optional one-time purchase, "Remove Ads", processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details; Google handles the transaction under its own policies. The purchase is tied to your Google Play account and can be restored from the app's Settings.

Data retention

Your game data remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app. Data held by Google as an advertising and billing provider follows Google's own retention rules.

Children

This app is intended for a general audience; it is not directed at children.

Your choices and contact

You may decline optional advertising consent where offered, remove ads with the one-time purchase, or uninstall the app to remove local records. Android device settings may offer advertising privacy controls. If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can contact us at androadel2014@gmail.com.
